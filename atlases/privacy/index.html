Privacy and analytics

Updated October 11, 2026

Understanding how the resources are used

This website uses Google Analytics to understand page visits and use of the learning resources. Google Analytics can use cookies and process browser, device and usage information under Google’s privacy policy.

The shared site tag sends page URLs without query strings or fragments. It does not send calculator selections, written notes or search text as custom events. Additional automatic measurement depends on the Google Analytics property settings.

Learning-tool inputs

The atlases are educational tools. TNM learning notes are stored locally in the browser; the staging calculation runs in the browser. Please use fictional examples and do not enter identifiable patient information.

Your choices

The shared tag does not load when your browser exposes an enabled Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal. You can also block analytics scripts, manage cookies in your browser, or use Google’s Analytics opt-out add-on. The learning tools work without analytics.

For questions or corrections, use the contact details on Satvik Tripathi’s website.
