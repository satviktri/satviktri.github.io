Sources & model notes

What this atlas is

The rotatable body map is an original, hand-authored teaching schematic. It is not a segmented CT/MRI dataset or a clinically validated anatomical reconstruction. Vessel positions, diameters and distances are simplified. It covers major named vessels, not every branch, capillary or anatomical variant.

Flow paths are ordered in normal physiological direction. White arrows remain visible when animation is paused. Particle speed does not represent velocity, pressure, pulse timing or measured perfusion. Arteries and veins are named by direction relative to the heart; color indicates relative oxygenation.

Paired tibial veins, palmar arches and pulmonary vein pairs are grouped. The intracranial arterial network, small visceral branches, capillaries, portosystemic collaterals and cardiac conduction system are not reconstructed. Coronary geometry shows one right-dominant pattern.
