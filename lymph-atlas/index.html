Sources & model notes

This first edition is a learning model of major lymphatic structures. Node clusters represent groups, not individual counts. Courses and organ shapes are schematic; the model is not derived from patient imaging or intended for diagnosis, staging, or treatment planning.

Drainage routes show common teaching patterns. Terminal ducts and tributaries vary, and some trunks drain directly into veins. Regional anatomy is more complex than the pathways shown here.

Design & software

Interaction concept inspired by NeuroAxis. This atlas has independently authored code, text, and schematic geometry. 3D rendering uses Three.js, MIT licensed; its license is included with the source.
