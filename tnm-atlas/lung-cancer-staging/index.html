Start with the correct staging system

This guide uses AJCC Version 9 for lung, introduced in 2025 and listed in AJCC’s 2026 current systems document. AJCC updates are disease-specific; this label does not mean that every organ uses the same edition. [1]

Use this guide for learning initial NSCLC staging. The calculator does not provide post-neoadjuvant or recurrent stage groups, treatment recommendations or independent clinical validation.

Build T, N and M separately

T

Primary extent

Consider size and invasion. A higher local-extension feature can override a size-only category.

N

Regional stations

N2a involves one ipsilateral mediastinal/subcarinal station; N2b involves multiple such stations. Count stations, not individual nodes.

M

Distant distribution

M1c1 means multiple extrathoracic metastases in one organ system; M1c2 involves multiple organ systems. [2]

Three worked examples

Example 1 · Initial clinical

One station

A 2.5 cm primary with no higher T feature, one involved ipsilateral mediastinal station and no distant metastasis.

cT1c cN2a cM0

Example 2 · Initial clinical

Two stations

Example 3 · Initial clinical

Distant bone lesions

How to use the calculator

  1. Open TNMAtlas and keep Lung selected.
  2. Choose the appropriate classification and initial assessment context.
  3. Enter the established T, N and M categories. Leave uncertain findings unresolved rather than assuming they are negative.
  4. Compare the result and explanation with the cited source. Record the edition and evidence behind the categories.

Use the atlas’s fictional examples to practise. Do not enter identifiable patient information into learning notes.
