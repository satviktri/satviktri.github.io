THE STAGING WORKFLOW

Build the stage from evidence.

A tumor’s diameter is only one part of the story. TNM categories are combined by a site-specific rule, not added numerically.

01

Confirm site & histology

A renal cell carcinoma is not staged like renal pelvis urothelial cancer. Exocrine pancreatic carcinoma is not a pancreatic neuroendocrine tumor.

02

Choose the system & timing

Use the correct edition for the diagnosis date and jurisdiction. This atlas implements the displayed AJCC version, not a universal UICC TNM9 system.

03

Assign T, N and M

Use the relevant examination, imaging, operative and pathology findings. T measures local extent; N describes the site’s regional basin; M describes distant disease.

04

Add required modifiers

Prostate needs PSA and Grade Group. Differentiated thyroid needs age at diagnosis. Breast prognostic staging needs biomarkers and grade beyond this anatomic module.

05

Apply the stage grouping

Match the full combination to the site’s rules. Review discordant or incomplete findings. Stage IV need not mean M1, and M1 need not always mean IV.

06

Document the reasoning

Record site, histology, edition, classification, categories, stage and evidence. Keep the original diagnosis stage when describing subsequent treatment response.

Read the prefix before the number.

c

Clinical

Initial assessment before treatment, including examination, imaging and diagnostic biopsies. A biopsy does not automatically make the whole case pathological.

p

Pathological

Appropriate surgical/pathological assessment combined with the clinical evidence. A negative distant assessment is cM0: pM0 is not a valid category.

yc / yp

Post-neoadjuvant

After initial systemic/radiation treatment, assessed clinically or after surgery. This calculator does not assign post-neoadjuvant stage groups.

Stage ≠ grade ≠ treatment response.

Stage describes disease extent and, in some systems, prognostic factors. Grade describes tumor differentiation or aggressiveness. RECIST describes changes in tumor measurements over time. None can substitute for the others.

Sources & scope

What was built

This is an independently authored learning atlas. Its rule engine maps explicitly selected categories to the displayed stage groups. Helpers provide selected size/depth rules; they do not establish a diagnosis or exhaust every descriptor. Original SVG diagrams and fictional examples were created for this project.

AJCC’s 2026 list identifies lung as Version 9 and the other included sites as 8th edition. AJCC Version 9 is released by disease site; it is not the same label as the UICC 9th edition. Verify the edition appropriate to the diagnosis date.

Breast output is anatomic only. Prostate output incorporates PSA and Grade Group; differentiated thyroid output incorporates age. No post-neoadjuvant/recurrent groups, treatment recommendations, survival estimates, automated image interpretation or patient-record analysis are provided.

Implementation & limitations

Definitions are concise teaching summaries, with detailed subcategories grouped where noted. Official manuals and source tables remain the reference for rare combinations and full descriptor lists. Missing inputs are not treated as negative. Biopsy confirmation of metastasis is required before the exporter labels it pM1.

No AJCC/NCI table layout, logo or illustration is reproduced. The content, example narratives, diagrams and code were authored independently; staging concepts and classification names are attributed to their sources. This app is not an AJCC, ACS or NCI product or endorsement, and has not undergone independent clinical validation.
